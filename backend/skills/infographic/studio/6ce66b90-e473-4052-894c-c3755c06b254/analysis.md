## Main Topic
This research paper presents how Google's Gemini 2.5 Pro model, using a self-verification pipeline with careful prompt design, successfully solved 5 out of 6 problems from the IMO 2025 competition, demonstrating significant advances in automated mathematical reasoning.

3. Why IMO serves as a rigorous benchmark for AI reasoning capabilities beyond traditional math benchmarks
