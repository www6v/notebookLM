# Style Reference: Editorial Modern

Use a clean modern editorial style focused on clarity, hierarchy, and polished information design.

## Visual Traits

- Crisp layout edges
- Calm contemporary palette
- Strong whitespace management
- Refined icons and diagram shapes

## Suitable Content

- Business explainers
- Product strategy summaries
- Technical overviews
- Data-informed storytelling

## Typography

- Bold, clean headline typography
- Minimal secondary styling
- High contrast between section titles and body labels

## Illustration Guidance

- Prefer flat vector elements, charts, and structured diagram components.
- Keep iconography consistent and understated.
- Let layout hierarchy do most of the visual work.

## Failure Modes To Avoid

- Decorative excess
- Mixed skeuomorphic and flat styles
- Overloaded gradients
- Dense captions that break the clean editorial look
