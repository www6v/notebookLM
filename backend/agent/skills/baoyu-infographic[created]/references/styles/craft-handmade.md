# Style Reference: Craft Handmade

Use a hand-crafted editorial illustration style with warmth and clarity.

## Visual Traits

- Hand-drawn or paper-cutout feel
- Organic shapes and friendly geometry
- Warm palette with soft contrast
- Playful but still information-dense

## Suitable Content

- Educational explainers
- Research summaries
- Human-centered narratives
- Topics that benefit from approachability

## Typography

- Readable, slightly characterful titles
- Clean body text
- Strong size contrast between title, section heads, and labels

## Illustration Guidance

- Prefer simple icons, symbols, arrows, and diagrammatic accents.
- Keep figures and objects stylized rather than realistic.
- Use highlights to draw attention to keywords and section anchors.

## Failure Modes To Avoid

- Photo-realistic rendering
- Hyper-glossy 3D effects
- Inconsistent illustration styles
- Overly cute visuals that reduce clarity
